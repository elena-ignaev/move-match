Dance practice with a camera

Learn a routine section by section, then see where your attempt differed.

Learning choreography from a video is hard: you can't see yourself, it's easy to mirror the wrong side, and "it looks off" doesn't tell you what to fix. MoveMatch lets you slow down and loop a section, record yourself dancing along with a ghost skeleton, replay your attempt next to the reference, and get specific notes: when you were late, and which joints differed at key moments.
